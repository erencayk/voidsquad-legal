Void Squad – Support

Void Squad is an iPhone strategy-action game: lead a squad of soldiers down the road, pick the right gates, defeat the mutant hordes and bosses.

Need help?

For questions, bug reports, purchase problems or feedback, email cayikeren@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Common questions

I lost my progress. Progress is stored on your device only. Deleting the app or switching phones without a device backup removes it.

I bought coins but did not receive them. Open the Shop and tap “Restore Purchases”, or email us with your Apple receipt.

Refunds. Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
