Void Squad – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains how the iPhone game “Void Squad” (the “App”) handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, coins, upgrades, settings and purchase status) is stored locally on your device. We cannot access it. It is removed when you delete the App.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple may share limited transaction information with us (for example, that a purchase was completed) according to Apple’s own privacy policy.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third parties

The App does not include third-party SDKs that collect data, and it does not share data with anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
