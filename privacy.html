Void Squad – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy explains how the iPhone game “Void Squad” (the “App”) handles information.

Summary

  • We (the developer) do not collect personal information. The App has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics of our own.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob collects some device and usage data to show and measure ads (details below).
  • The App does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and it does not ask for Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission. Apple’s Advertising Identifier (IDFA) is not accessed.
  • Game progress is stored only on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays full-screen ads between levels and optional rewarded ads (for example, “watch an ad to double your coins”). These ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers: an app-specific identifier (Identifier for Vendors) and similar technical identifiers. The IDFA is not used because the App does not request tracking permission.
  • Advertising data: which ads were shown, clicked or completed.
  • Usage and diagnostic data: app interactions related to ads, app launch information, crash and performance data of the ads SDK.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address (not precise GPS location).
  • Device information: device model, operating system version, language and network type.

This data is processed by Google under its own policies. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App shows Google’s consent form before any ads are requested. You can accept or decline personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Privacy Choices (Ads). If you decline, you may still see non-personalized ads, which use data only for limited purposes such as frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement.

On any iPhone you can also turn off personalized ads from Apple’s side under Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising, and you can reset or stop the App by deleting it.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, coins, upgrades, settings, daily reward streak and purchase status) is stored locally on your device. We cannot access it. It is removed when you delete the App.

In-app purchases

Purchases (coin packs and “Remove Ads”) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple may share limited transaction information with us (for example, that a purchase was completed) according to Apple’s own privacy policy. “Remove Ads” removes ads shown between levels; optional rewarded ads stay available if you choose to watch them.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and is not listed in the App Store Kids category. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and deletion

We do not hold any of your data on our own servers. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google’s policies. To remove the data stored by the App on your device, delete the App.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
